How it works

  1. Click anywhere on this page. Anywhere at all, there are no buttons.
  2. Your counter goes up by 1 and is saved in this browser, so it is still here when you come back.
  3. The game opens itself in a new tab, so you can keep going.
  4. The tab you clicked in jumps to an ad. It is a random pick between two ad links.
  5. Close the ad, go back to the game tab, and click again. Climb the ranks!

Ranks

    Every click sends you to a real ad, which is how this site earns money. Your score lives only in your browser (localStorage). Clearing your site data resets it.
